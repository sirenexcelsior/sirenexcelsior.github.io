## Anatoly Alexandrovich Chasovskikh

**Professor; Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences**

Anatoly Chasovskikh’s research interests include discrete control systems, automata theory, pattern recognition, artificial neural networks, graph theory and cryptography.

He solved the completeness problem for classes of linear automata over finite fields and identified all precomplete classes. He developed approaches to estimating the complexity of neural circuits in bases associated with the McCulloch–Pitts neuron model. He also developed technological solutions for automatically detecting and classifying objects in images under specified constraints on imaging conditions.

His official biography lists six books, 32 articles and three researchers whom he supervised to the Candidate of Sciences degree.
