## Dmitry Vladimirovich Alexeev

**Associate Professor; Candidate of Physical and Mathematical Sciences; Coordinator for Postgraduate Studies**

Dmitry Alexeev graduated from MSU’s Advanced Educational and Scientific Centre in 1990 and its Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics in 1995. He received his Candidate of Sciences degree in 2006 for a dissertation on approximating functions of one and several real variables with the Chebyshev–Hermite weight.

His interests include function theory, geometric approaches to pattern recognition, coding theory and erasure codes. His results include direct and inverse approximation theorems, an inverse theorem on reconstructing a solid from planar projections, and an existence theorem for an image with a prescribed code.

He has worked at MSU’s Laboratory of Theoretical Cybernetics since 1997 and the Centre’s Mathematics Department since 2000. In 2015, he won the “Mentor of Future Scientists” award in the national teachers’ competition organised by the Dynasty and Modern Natural Science foundations.

His teaching covers discrete analysis, intelligent systems, mathematical cybernetics, algebra, C++ system design and computer practicals; he also co-teaches mathematical biology with Vadim Kozlov. His official biography lists 32 articles, seven books, three US patents and nine supervised graduation theses.
