## Vadim Nikitovich Kozlov

**Professor; Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences; Corresponding Member of the Academy of Technological Sciences of the Russian Federation**

Vadim Kozlov received his Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences degree in 1997. His doctoral dissertation was entitled “Mathematical Modelling of Visual Perception and Recognition Processes.” Until 1999, he was an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematical Cybernetics at the Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics, Moscow State University. Since 1999, he has been a Professor in the Department of Mathematical Theory of Intelligent Systems at MSU’s Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics.

His research focuses on visual pattern recognition and mathematical modelling in biology. He leads research seminars on mathematical modelling in biology and pattern recognition.
